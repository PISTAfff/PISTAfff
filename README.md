## // 01 · WHO

Hi, I'm **PISTA**. I make games, ship full-stack tools, and turn off-hour curiosity into something that actually runs. I care about *game feel* and the product that ships — small things, often, with custom tools when the off-the-shelf ones stop fitting.

- 🎮 Mostly **2D pixel art**, occasionally **3D** when an idea insists
- 🧩 Also full-stack web, Android apps, and the occasional embedded toy
- 🏢 Currently **part-time @ REACTEG** (formerly Sonoff Egypt) — RFID software, company website, order pipeline
- 🎓 Studying at **NCTU** · ex-MICA · ex-MICA instructor
- 🌍 Earth · GMT+3 · **open to freelance · remote gigs · gamejams · collabs**
